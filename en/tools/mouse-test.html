Mechanical Switch Chatter vs Optical Switches & USB Polling Rate

Why aging mice double click and how 1000Hz to 8000Hz polling rates impact gaming precision.

Why Do Mice Double Click?

Traditional mechanical mice rely on metal leaf spring contacts (such as Omron or TTC switches). Over time, metal fatigue, oxidation, and humidity cause electrical contact resistance to fluctuate. When you press the button once, the spring physically bounces multiple times within a few milliseconds (contact bounce / chatter). Once the debounce filter fails to suppress these bounces, the OS registers an unintended double click.

Optical switches (like Razer Optical or Logitech Lightforce) replace physical contact points with infrared light beams, completely eliminating mechanical contact wear and making unintentional double-clicks physically impossible.
