Display Panel Tech (IPS vs OLED vs Mini-LED) & ISO 9241 Pixel Standards

Understanding backlight bleed, GtG response times, and standard return policies for defective pixels.

1. Dead Pixels vs Stuck Pixels

Dead Pixels: A pixel that remains permanently off, appearing as a tiny black dot against bright or white backgrounds. Dead pixels cannot be revived.

Stuck Pixels: A subpixel (Red, Green, or Blue) stuck in an energized state, appearing as a colored pinpoint against black backgrounds.

According to ISO 9241-307 Class II (standard for consumer monitors and laptops), panels are typically allowed up to 2 permanently bright pixels, 2 permanently dark pixels, or 5 defective subpixels per million pixels before being considered eligible for warranty replacement.
